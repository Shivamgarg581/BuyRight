METHOD

How BuyRight knows what it knows.

Separate observations, calculations and interpretation.

1 · OFFICIAL

Reference layer

The shipped reference dataset is the dated Department of Consumer Affairs Price Monitoring snapshot. The source date is preserved and the daily workflow refuses to publish when parsing looks incomplete.

2 · LOCAL

Observation layer

Local prices are community observations with a market label, quantity, unit and observation date. They are separate from official references and only verified GitHub reports are promoted.

3 · ANALYSIS

Deterministic engine

Unit normalization, basket totals, MRP comparisons and historical statistics are calculations, not AI guesses.

4 · UNCERTAINTY

Unknown is valid

No verified local evidence means unknown. A stale or missing source is never silently relabeled as live.
