# BuyRight data sources

## Department of Consumer Affairs — Price Monitoring System
Official daily monitoring for selected essential commodities. Use it as a national retail/wholesale reference, not as a live quote for every local shop.

https://fcainfoweb.nic.in/Default.aspx

## e-NAM
National Agriculture Market portal for agricultural market/trading context.

https://enam.gov.in/dashboard

## AGMARKNET
Agricultural market/mandi price and arrivals information.

https://agmarknet.gov.in/

## Local observations
BuyRight can accept shop/user reports. These must be labeled as local/community data and should support moderation, evidence, timestamp and source metadata.

## Data contract
Every price record needs: product definition, quantity, unit, normalized price, market/location, source, observed_at, verification_status.
